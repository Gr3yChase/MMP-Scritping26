About Edward

Edward Elric is a smart and adaptable kid from the town of Resembool. Once an average child of humble beginnings, tragedy struck when Edward's mother passed away leaving them alone as their father had left years ago. Edward and his brother Alphonse attempted to use the power of this world that they were born with called Alchemy. Their attempt of bringing their mother back left permanent scars on themselves but on their journey to find answers they would be caught in the middle of the greatest conspiracy in Amestris.

His Impact on Amestris

The conspiracy Edward and his brother Alphonse found themselves to be in would be a far larger issue than they realized far too late. The conspiracy threatens to destroy the country and on their journey to find answers for their mother's failed revival it led them to knowing things others would prefer they did not know. They expose government experiments involving human sacrifice and things called Homunculus pulling the strings of their entire country and orchestrating a large ritual that would lead to sacrificing all the people living in Amestris. In their travels they saved many but also accidentally caused a rebellion, they assisted others struggling with loss or issues that they were facing or just helping others that would help their goals but in the end they didn't let their goal get in the way of what was important and that was helping whoever they could and in the end saving the world.

Personality & Character

Edward is very strong willed and at his core a very kind, genuine man, willing to sacrifice his goals and ambitions to help others but his strongest attribute is his sheer dedication. Losing an arm and a leg didn't stop him from pursuing recovering his brother's body, for the most part this mission was fueled by guilt and a sense of responsibility but not even his own brother's doubts of achieving their goal made him stop. Edward is a downright prodigy of the craft of Alchemy, the practice of constructing and destroying matter at your will. At a young age he learned alchemy with mere books and he gained enough to try human transmutation which is something older alchemists never dream of doing. Another thing he did that most don't achieve until adulthood is becoming a registered alchemist with the military at the young age of 14 due to his talent with the craft and theoretical practices he tends to put into effect quite often.

Abilities & Skills

First of all Edward is very agile, fast and strong. Constantly maintaining his body with working out and constantly practicing his hand to hand combat capabilities through vigorous training and practice with his brother. What also makes him strong is his fully metal arm and leg that he can manipulate to create weapons through the use of alchemy and his specialty is metal alchemy changing all sorts of metal into all sorts of constructs. Alchemy is the main tool Edward and others use to fight, people who have access to this are called "Alchemist". Edward's understanding of Alchemy goes way beyond any average person which makes him an effective fighter.

Important Relationships

Though Edward's mother is a massive part of his journey we don't know much about her in detail or her relationship with Edward and others. Alphonse is the infamous tall robot looking thing that is always seen with Edward also known as his brother. Alphonse is a very important part of Edward's life as gaining back Alphonse's body that they lost trying to get their mother back is practically the driving force to keep going the entire story. Their bond is stronger than any metal they have manipulated and their will combined is a terrifying force. Winry Rockbell is a mutual friend of both Alphonse and Edward as they all grew together when Edward and Alphonse moved into her house after their mother's passing. Winry is a automail engineer and is actually the one that gave and maintains Edward's Automail arm and leg. Throughout the story there is a tension between the two that is finally resolved and with this resolved they finally realize they like one another and marry each other. Finally the father of Edward that left is actually a Van Hohenheim and he has been around for hundreds of years. He may have left but he admits he was truly in love with Edward's mother and he has been working to prevent the destruction of Amestris that Edward finds out about later in the show.
