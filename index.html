About

Hi! I'm Chase, but online I also go by Gr3y_1. I started my journey in creative game development around three years ago when I discovered Roblox Studio. At the time, it was convenient and easy to get into because many of my friends were playing Roblox. What started as an interest in games eventually became an interest in creating my own content.

My first project was a recreation of Christophsis from Star Wars. Although the project wasn't very impressive visually, it gave me my first real experience with game development. I needed to create the map layout and build assets that resembled the original environment.

During this project, I began learning Blender to create 3D models and improve the assets used in my games. My second project focused on improving what I had previously created, particularly through better modelling and more detailed texture design. Over time, I became more interested in hard-surface modelling, including vehicles, weapons, armour and other game assets.

Since then, I have taken on numerous commissions involving a wide variety of challenges in 3D modelling and texturing. These projects have allowed me to practise different techniques and continue developing my skills. In the future, I hope to expand my abilities beyond modelling and texturing by learning more about rigging, sculpting and animation.
