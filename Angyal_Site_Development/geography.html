Geography of Taber

Taber, true to its surroundings, is a very flat town. If you were to enter or exit Taber you would only see maybe a few farmhouses in the distance but by a major margin you would observe plains filled with corn, beets and potatoes that stretch as far as the eye can see. Taber, in recent time has really taken advantage of their local coulees. The coulees offer wonderful sights, great plant life and wildlife. If you were to visit the Taber coulees you would first find yourself on the one of the many, but beautiful nature walks that the coulee offers. If you’re more into bike riding, the coulees have a vast network of bike trails both beginner friendly and some even veterans would think twice in trying
