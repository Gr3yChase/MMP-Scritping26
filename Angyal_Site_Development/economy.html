Economy of Taber

One of the biggest contributurs and supporters of our local community is the sugar beat fectory. The factory si always offering new jobs to the people of taber while also bring plenty of business to the town.

The pride and joy of Taber is our farmers. The people of taber are fond of the flat lands around them and no one uses it better then Taber farmers. Local farmers are always hiring and their crop bring plenty of foods to local gorcery stores and markets.

A silent king, a Oil King! Taber oil economy may be small in comparison to other but a proud part of our community are oil patch workers. Taber is proud to contrinue oil to the industry and produce the great oil patch men and women you see today.

Workers of Taber

Besides either being a farmer or working for one, most Taber residents are blue collar workers either working in construction or the oil patch. If you go to the bar in Taber on Saturday night you will find no shortage of oil patch workers. If you’re coming to Taber to find a crew to build something for you, there is no shortage of local companies and blue-collar workers to help you make your dream project a reality. This mighty town of Taber has one of the strongest local business support networks in the area, if you start a business in Taber, you should find no shortage of customers as Taber residents are proud to shop locally and it's well-advertised throughout the town.
