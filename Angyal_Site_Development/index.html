About Taber

Taber, a place of protest and corn. A destination ideal for corn lovers and people who are displeased with the government, no town is better in southern Alberta then Taber. The smell of prairie sage attacks your nose at every step and every August the sweet taste of corn invades your taste buds. Every winter the great feeling of adrenaline enters the communitys hearts as the puck drops on its first ice and the sweet sting of winter pinches their cheeks, Winter is the ideal time in Taber.
